RAGAS (RAG Assessment) evaluates RAG pipelines without human labels:
- Uses LLM-as-judge for faithfulness, relevancy, precision, recall
- Requires: question, answer, contexts, ground_truth (optional)
- Faithfulness: decompose answer into claims, verify each against context
- Answer Relevancy: generate questions from answer, compare to original
- Context Precision: rank contexts by relevance to question
- Context Recall: check if ground truth info present in contexts
- Outputs: per-query scores + aggregate statistics
