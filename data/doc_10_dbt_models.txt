dbt (data build tool) transforms raw data into analytics-ready marts:
- Staging models: clean, typed, renamed columns from raw Parquet
- Intermediate models: business logic, aggregations, joins
- Mart models: final fact/dimension tables for dashboards
- Tests: not_null, unique, accepted_values, relationships
- Docs: auto-generated data catalog
- Jinja macros: reusable SQL snippets

Models are SELECT statements; dbt handles DAG execution, incremental builds.
