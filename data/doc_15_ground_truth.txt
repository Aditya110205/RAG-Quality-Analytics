Ground truth dataset enables quantitative evaluation:
- Curated Q&A pairs from domain experts
- Each entry: question, answer, relevant_doc_ids, relevant_chunk_ids
- Minimum 50-100 questions for statistical significance
- Covers: factoid, multi-hop, negative (unanswerable), ambiguous
- Version controlled with code (data/ground_truth.jsonl)
- Used for: hit rate, MRR, answer correctness, regression testing

Without ground truth, you only have proxy metrics (heuristics).
