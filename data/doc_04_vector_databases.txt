Vector databases store and index embeddings for fast similarity search. Types:
- Chroma: Lightweight, embedded, great for prototyping. Supports metadata filtering.
- Pinecone: Managed, scalable, enterprise features.
- Weaviate: GraphQL, hybrid search, modules for ML.
- Qdrant: Rust-based, high performance, filtering.
- Milvus: Distributed, billion-scale, multiple index types.
- FAISS: Library (not DB), fast ANN search, need custom serving.

Index types: HNSW (hierarchical navigable small world) - most popular, good recall/speed tradeoff. IVF (inverted file) - partition-based, faster build. PQ (product quantization) - compresses vectors.
