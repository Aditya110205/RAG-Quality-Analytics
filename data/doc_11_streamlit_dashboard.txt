Streamlit builds interactive data apps in pure Python:
- st.line_chart, st.bar_chart, st.area_chart for quick plots
- st.plotly_chart for interactive Plotly figures
- st.dataframe for sortable, filterable tables
- st.selectbox, st.slider, st.date_input for controls
- st.cache_data, st.cache_resource for performance
- Session state for multi-page apps

Pages: Overview (volume, latency), Quality (RAGAS scores), Retrieval (hit rate, MRR), Experiments (config comparison).
